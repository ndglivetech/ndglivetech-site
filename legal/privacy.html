2. Personal Data Collected

NDG Live Tech may collect personal data, in particular when you:

  • use the website contact form;
  • contact NDG Live Tech by email or telephone;
  • request or accept a quotation;
  • order a service or rental;
  • participate in a project, event or production;
  • request an audiovisual service or drone mission;
  • browse the website, where you have accepted the relevant analytics tools.

Depending on the circumstances, the data processed may include:

  • first and last name;
  • email address;
  • telephone number;
  • postal address;
  • company or organisation name;
  • company number and billing information;
  • information relating to the project, event or intervention site;
  • content of messages and communications;
  • photographs, videos or other content required to perform a service;
  • technical data and information relating to the use of the website.

3. Purposes and Legal Bases

NDG Live Tech processes personal data only where there is a legal basis permitting such processing.

Processing may in particular be necessary in order to:

  • respond to a request and prepare a quotation or service — pre-contractual measures;
  • organise, perform and follow up services — performance of a contract;
  • issue and manage invoices, payments and accounting documents — performance of the contract and legal obligations;
  • comply with accounting, tax or regulatory obligations applicable to NDG Live Tech — legal obligation;
  • ensure administrative management, service security and the defence of NDG Live Tech's rights — legitimate interest;
  • analyse the use of the website where the user's consent is required and has been given — consent.

4. Contact Form

The NDG Live Tech website uses a third-party technical service to enable contact forms to be submitted.

Information entered into the form is processed in order to transmit and handle your request. You are advised not to provide sensitive data or information that is not necessary for your request through the contact form.

5. Data Related to Audiovisual and Drone Services

In connection with audiovisual, event or drone services, NDG Live Tech may process photographs, videos and other images required to perform the mission.

Depending on the circumstances, these images may allow persons to be identified directly or indirectly and may therefore constitute personal data.

NDG Live Tech limits the collection and use of these images to what is necessary for the purpose of the service, applicable regulatory obligations and the uses agreed with the Client.

During a drone inspection, images may in particular show buildings, land, vehicles or surrounding elements. NDG Live Tech takes reasonable measures to avoid the unnecessary collection of data relating to third parties.

6. Google Analytics and Browsing Data

NDG Live Tech uses Google Analytics to better understand how the website is used, including the number of visits, pages viewed and certain technical information relating to the devices and browsers used.

Where legislation requires the user's consent, the relevant analytics technologies are used only in accordance with the choice expressed by the user through the cookie management system.

Further information about cookies and similar technologies is available in the Cookies Policy.

7. Recipients and Service Providers

Personal data is not sold to third parties.

It may, however, be disclosed where necessary to service providers acting on behalf of NDG Live Tech, including in the following areas:

  • website hosting and technical services;
  • forms and electronic communications;
  • website analytics tools;
  • billing and accounting;
  • technical service providers and subcontractors required for a service;
  • professional advisers;
  • public authorities where required by law.

These recipients have access to the data only to the extent necessary to perform their tasks or comply with their obligations.

8. International Data Transfers

Certain technical service providers used by NDG Live Tech may process or make data accessible from countries outside the European Economic Area.

Where such transfers take place, NDG Live Tech ensures, to the extent required by applicable legislation, that they are based on a recognised legal mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards.

9. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, as well as for the periods necessary to comply with legal obligations or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Accounting and tax documents are retained for the periods required by applicable legislation.

Data relating to contact requests that do not result in a commercial relationship is not intended to be retained indefinitely.

Images, videos, reports and other files relating to a service are retained for the period necessary to perform and follow up that service, unless longer retention is justified by the contract, a legal obligation or a legitimate interest.

10. Data Security

NDG Live Tech implements reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or unauthorised disclosure.

As no computer system or means of transmission can provide absolute security, these measures are reasonably adapted to the nature of the data and the identified risks.

11. Your Rights

Subject to the conditions laid down by the GDPR, you may in particular have the following rights:

  • the right to be informed about the processing of your data;
  • the right of access to your personal data;
  • the right to rectify inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • the right to erasure where the legal conditions are met;
  • the right to restriction of processing;
  • the right to object to certain processing;
  • the right to data portability where applicable;
  • the right to withdraw your consent where processing is based on consent;
  • the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing which produces certain legal or similarly significant effects, where this right applies.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal.
