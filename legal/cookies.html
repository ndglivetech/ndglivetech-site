1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can be used to make a website work properly, improve user experience, remember preferences or collect basic statistics.

5. Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies, delete existing cookies or receive a warning before cookies are stored.
