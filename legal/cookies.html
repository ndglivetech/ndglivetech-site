Cookies Policy

This Cookies Policy explains how NDG Live Tech uses cookies and similar technologies on its website, as well as the choices available to visitors regarding their use.

Last updated — September 2026

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file or piece of information stored on or read from your device when you visit a website.

Cookies and similar technologies may, in particular, enable certain website features, remember choices or measure website traffic and use.

2. Categories of cookies used

The NDG Live Tech website may use different categories of cookies and similar technologies.

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are necessary for the operation of the website or for the provision of a service expressly requested by the user. Where they are strictly necessary, they may be used without prior consent.

Analytics cookies

NDG Live Tech uses Google Analytics to obtain statistics about the use of the website and to improve its content, navigation and performance.

Where their use requires your consent, these technologies are activated only in accordance with the choice you have expressed through the cookie management system.

3. Google Analytics

The website uses Google Analytics, an analytics service provided by Google.

This service enables NDG Live Tech to understand statistically how the website is used, for example which pages are visited and how visitors use the website.

As part of this service, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and process certain technical information relating to your visit, browser and device.

The use of Google Analytics is linked to the consent choice expressed by the visitor where such consent is required.

4. Cookies and third-party services

Certain technical services used by NDG Live Tech may also be involved in the operation of the website or in processing certain requests.

Where a third-party service uses cookies or similar technologies that are not strictly necessary, their use is subject to the applicable consent requirements.

5. Your choices regarding cookies

Where consent is required, you can accept or reject the use of the relevant cookies through the cookie banner or cookie management tool displayed on the website.

Rejecting non-essential cookies should not prevent access to the website's essential features.

You can also delete cookies that have already been stored or change certain permissions through your browser settings.

6. Withdrawal or modification of consent

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw or modify that consent at any time.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal.

You can also delete cookies already stored on your device through your browser settings.

7. Retention period

The retention period depends on the type of cookie or technology used.

NDG Live Tech ensures that cookies and associated data are not retained beyond the period necessary for the purposes for which they are used, subject to the retention periods applied by the relevant service providers and applicable legislation.

8. Personal data

Where the use of cookies or similar technologies results in the processing of personal data, such processing is also subject to NDG Live Tech's Privacy Policy.

This policy contains, in particular, information concerning the purposes of processing, recipients of the data and the rights available to data subjects.

9. Changes to the Cookies Policy

NDG Live Tech may amend this Cookies Policy to take account of changes to the website, the services used or applicable regulations.

The date of the latest update is indicated at the top of this page.
