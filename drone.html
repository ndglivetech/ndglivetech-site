A PRACTICAL INSPECTION SOLUTION

Inspect first. Decide with better information.

Drone inspection provides fast visual access to elevated or difficult areas without requiring scaffolding, ladders or direct access for an initial assessment.

Before the Flight

Every inspection starts with the right checks.

Before each mission, the location and operational environment are reviewed to confirm that the inspection can be carried out safely and legally.

This includes airspace, weather conditions, site access, applicable flight restrictions and privacy considerations.

If specific authorisations or additional arrangements are required, these are identified before the inspection and discussed with you in advance.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need to be present during the inspection?

Not always. This depends on the property, access requirements and the areas to be inspected. If access can be arranged in advance, the inspection can often be carried out without you being present.

What happens if the weather is unsuitable?

If wind, rain or visibility conditions do not allow the flight to be carried out safely, the inspection will be postponed or rescheduled.

Can every property be inspected?

Most locations can be assessed, but airspace restrictions, site conditions, privacy considerations or other operational constraints may require additional preparation or limit the inspection.

Can you inspect a roof without climbing onto it?

In many cases, yes. The drone can provide detailed exterior imagery of roofs and difficult-to-access areas without requiring direct roof access for the initial visual inspection.

What will I receive after the inspection?

This depends on the selected package. Deliverables can include high-resolution photographs, organised photographic documentation and, when selected, a structured PDF inspection report.

How quickly will I receive the results?

Delivery time depends on the selected package and the scope of the inspection. The expected delivery time is confirmed before the mission.

Can the report be shared with a roofer or insurance company?

Yes. The digital documentation can be shared with roofers, contractors, property managers, insurers or other professionals. The report provides visual documentation and does not replace a structural or specialist diagnosis.
